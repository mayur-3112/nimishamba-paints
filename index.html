We believe the right colour does more than decorate a wall — it changes how a space feels, how a family lives, and how a home becomes truly yours.

Nimishamba Paints was founded in 2005 with one goal: to bring Mysuru the finest Berger paint products alongside genuine expertise and honest service. 20 years later, that mission hasn’t changed.
