Our Story

Mysuru’s most
trusted paint store.

Nimishamba Paints & Plywoods has been serving Mysuru since 2005. Located at Hinkal near Ring Road Circle, we’ve helped thousands of homeowners, interior designers, and contractors find the perfect Berger paint for every surface and space.

As an authorized dealer, we carry the full Berger range — from luxury interior emulsions to heavy-duty exterior coatings. Our staff can guide you on the right product, finish, and quantity for any project.
